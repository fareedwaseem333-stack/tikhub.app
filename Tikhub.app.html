FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the downloader free? +

This frontend is designed as a free-to-use interface. Any actual downloading service depends on the backend or media provider connected to it.

Do I need a TikTok account? +

The page itself does not ask for a TikTok username or password.

Where is the downloaded video saved? +

Your browser normally saves downloaded files to its configured Downloads folder.

Can I use it on Android or iPhone? +

Yes. The page is responsive and can be opened from a modern mobile browser.

Does the HTML file itself download TikTok videos? +

No. HTML and Tailwind create the interface. A real downloader requires a server-side download service or an authorized media provider.
